About Me

I am a passionate developer and nature lover. I am happiest when spending time in the mountains, the forest, or at my developer space.

Skills & Expertise

I am Master of Science, specialized on Biophysics and Biophysical Methods, as well as Artificial Intelligence. During my studies, I worked on many projects in life-sciences, e.g. Single Cell Tracking with High Resolution Microscopy, Biosensor Development, Drug Discovery, Robotics, MID-IR Spectroscopy and many more.

Project Overview

Prototyping is a real pleasure for me and I could practice it in many projects. From software development to hardware development, in life-sciences and healthcare, from microscopes to robots, in teams or alone. It is a real pleasure for me to see progress every day. My favorite part is trying out new things in the Makerspace.

Software Related Projects

As a Software Developer, I love working with sensor data. Transforming measurements into useful information is the key for decision-making. Developing models that can transform the sensor data into robotic actions, is the best way to substitute subjective human decisions with objective machine decisions and it guarantees reproducibility.

Hardware Related Projects

Starting early by repairing everything broken at home, from electronics, bikes, moped and cars, I continued to work on hardware projects in the laboratory and for entertainment. Results are advanced laser systems in microscopy, web-controlled robot arms and a glucose biosensor for diabetic detection. Additionally I worked on many projects for fun, entertainment and teaching, like a lensless microscope, a person-tracking drone and many more little projects.
